Frequently Asked Questions

What is Growy?

Growy is a productivity app for iPhone, Mac, and Android that helps you build habits, track goals, and stay focused. It combines a daily planner, focus timer, and progress tracker in one system. Free to download.

What platforms is Growy available on?

Growy is available on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android. Your goals and progress sync automatically across all devices.

How is Growy different from Todoist or Notion?

Todoist is a task manager. Notion is a workspace tool. Growy is a complete productivity system — it tracks your goals, builds your habits, and keeps you focused with a built-in timer. No setup, no complexity.

Does Growy help with focus and deep work?

Yes. Growy has a built-in Focus Timer that locks you into a single goal until the session ends. Users report 2x longer focus sessions and 94% say they feel more productive.

How do I stop procrastinating?

Growy helps you break your goals into daily steps so you always know exactly what to do next. The focus timer removes distractions so you can lock in without willpower.

Can Growy replace multiple productivity apps?

Yes. Growy replaces a habit tracker, goal planner, focus timer, and progress journal — four apps in one, free to download.

How long does it take to see results with Growy?

Most users report feeling more focused within the first week. On average, Growy users complete 4 more tasks per day compared to before using the app.

Who is Growy for?

Growy is for anyone who wants to be more productive — students, entrepreneurs, remote workers, and anyone who struggles with focus, procrastination, or staying consistent with their goals.
